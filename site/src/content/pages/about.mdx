I'm Doron Omer, a QA automation engineer based in Israel, with 20 years of experience in QA.

I spent my first years testing large systems, including trading platforms, GIS, and integrated hardware and software. About six years ago I moved from manual testing into automation, and since then I've built test frameworks from scratch and the infrastructure around them. In my most recent role I was the sole owner of test automation for a fintech platform: UI and API suites across several applications, the CI pipelines that ran them, and the reporting that made failures explainable.

Pagewright and the articles on this site come out of that work: a config-driven framework, simplified into a reusable template, and an honest account of the decisions behind it.

I'm Doron Omer, a QA automation engineer based in Israel, with six years of automation experience.

I started in manual testing and moved into automation after about a year and a half. Since then I've built test frameworks and the infrastructure around them. In my most recent role I was the sole owner of test automation for a fintech platform: UI and API suites across several applications, the CI pipelines that ran them, and the reporting that made failures explainable. That included testing AI-driven features, such as a conversational WhatsApp bot.

Pagewright and the articles on this site come out of that work: a config-driven framework rebuilt as an open template, and an honest account of the decisions behind it.

I'm currently looking for a senior automation or automation infrastructure role.
